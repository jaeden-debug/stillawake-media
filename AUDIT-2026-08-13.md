**What is NOT the reason:** technical SEO, page speed, schema, crawlability, or metadata. Those are measurably fine (§7). Anyone who tells you to fix your titles to rank for "web design montreal" is selling you busywork.

The French Québec market is **~25× smaller than the US** and carries **five HIGH-competition terms where the US set has none.** "developpement web montreal" scores competition index **100** on 10 searches/month — a maximally contested term with essentially no demand.

This does not mean abandon French. Your French content is original Québec-market writing (Loi 25, Loi 96) that no translated competitor can match, and it serves real clients. **But French should not be where you spend acquisition effort.** The evidence says English — and increasingly US English — is where the winnable volume is.

**Second counterintuitive finding:** the AI-automation cluster has simultaneously the **highest US volume growth** and the **lowest cost per click by an order of magnitude** ($1.08–3.36 vs $23–128 for custom software / Next.js terms). Markets do not usually offer high volume, low competition, and low CPC together. This one currently does — which implies it will not last.
