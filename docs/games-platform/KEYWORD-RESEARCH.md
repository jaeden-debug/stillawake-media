## What this means

1. **Brand searches don't exist yet.** "Stillskin Forest" has no measurable volume. The wiki's job right now is to be ready, indexed and correct when the game goes public, so that the first people who search for it land on official pages. That is what was built.
2. **The genre demand is large but it is list-intent.** "scary roblox games" and "roblox horror games" are searches for *lists of games*. **Published 2026-10-26, expanded 2026-10-29:** `/games/guides/scary-roblox-games-with-friends` (originally `/games/guides/roblox-survival-horror-games`, now a 301) compares fifteen established games; it began with eight (DOORS, The Mimic, Pressure, 3008, Dead Rails, Piggy, Forsaken, Apeirophobia). Every figure comes from Roblox's public listing for that game, dated. It isn't a ranking. It discloses that StillAwake makes Stillskin Forest and leaves the game off the list because it isn't publicly playable yet. Refresh the numbers periodically (see the data file's header).
3. **Co-op intent is the best fit.** "roblox games to play with friends" (6,600 US) matches the game's real differentiator. The landing page and "Playing with friends" guide are written to that intent.
