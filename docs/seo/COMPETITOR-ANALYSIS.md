# Competitor analysis (2026-10-26)

Source: Google web searches run on 2026-10-26 for the priority terms. Results are personalized and location-dependent, so treat them as the shape of each results page, not exact ranks. Ahrefs and Semrush, which would give competitors' traffic and links, weren't available (see `BACKLINK-AUDIT.md`).

## "seo montreal" / "seo agency montreal" (EN)

- **Agencies seen:** h1site, mylittlebigweb, Thrive, Mediaforce, Shooga, Let's Get Optimized, SEO Troop.
- **Listicles and directories:** Nuoptima "best SEO agencies" listicle, Semrush Agency Partners, Clutch.
- **Pattern:** directories and listicles take several top spots. Agencies that rank have years of history and many referring domains.
- **What they show on the page:** service list, case results, local address, reviews or ratings, a contact form above the fold.

## "agence seo montréal" (FR)

- **Agencies seen:** Digitad, Renaissance SEO (via Clutch), Boost One SEO, OptiWeb, GroupFractal, Hamak, Social Media 55.
- **Directories:** HelloDarwin and Clutch.
- **Pattern:** French results are less crowded, which matches StillAwake's better position here (9.7).

## "web design montreal" / "agence web montréal"

- **Agencies seen:** Digitad, The Influence Agency, Passionate, Plank, Motto.
- **Directories:** HelloDarwin listings.
- **Pattern:** these are established studios with large portfolios and press. StillAwake's page sits around position 96. Closing that gap needs authority (links, listings, Google Business Profile), not more copy.

## What StillAwake has that the results lack

- Published prices on every service page. Most competitors say "contact us".
- Bilingual pages with matching content.
- Dated, sourced case studies (TravelDesign By Lisa, BankDeMark, Blackwater Aquatics, Stalkr).

## What StillAwake lacks

- Directory listings (Clutch, HelloDarwin, Semrush Agency Partners), which are themselves ranking in the results.
- Google Business Profile visibility. No map-pack presence was observed.
- Reviews. None are published, and none should be invented.
- Referring domains. The count couldn't be measured, but the low impression base suggests very few.

## Implications

- **Directory listings are the fastest route onto these results pages**, because the directories already rank (B-06, owner action).
- **The French SEO page** is the most winnable page-1 target.
- **On-page work on web design pages** has diminishing returns until authority improves.
