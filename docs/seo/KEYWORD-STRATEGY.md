## Priorities

1. **SEO cluster (EN + FR).** About 650 searches a month across "seo montreal", "seo services montreal", "seo agency montreal" and "agence seo montréal". Both owner pages already sit closest to page 1 (14 and 9.7). Highest return per hour of work.
2. **Web design / création de site web.** About 350 a month combined, but the pages sit near position 90–100, so this is an authority problem (links, local signals) more than an on-page one.
3. **Price pages.** /fr/prix-site-web-quebec has the most impressions of any commercial page (42 at 14.2). Strengthened in PR #5.
4. **Everything else** (Shopify, software, branding) has too little measured demand to prioritize over the above.

## Not pursued

- City pages for suburbs (Laval, Longueuil, etc.). StillAwake has no offices there; separate near-duplicate pages would be doorway pages.
- High-volume generic terms ("website development" nationally). No realistic path without authority.
