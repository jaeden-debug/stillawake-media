## Name
RISK IT!

## Description (under Roblox's 1000-character limit)

Walk up. Risk it. Get launched. Try again.

A neon game show floating in the void: eight tiny challenges, 5 to 20 seconds each. Win and you bank GUTS. Fail and you get ragdolled into orbit.

THE CHALLENGES
🔴 THE BUTTON: every press doubles the pot. The odds are on the board.
🔵 PERFECT STOP: stop the needle in the zone. Three rounds, faster every time.
🟢 QUICK DRAW: wait for green. Flinch and the floor catapults you.
🟡 THE SWEEPER: cross the bridge. Jump the arms, time the wall.
🟣 DOOR NUMBER WHO: three doors, one trap. Watch the lights.
🟠 MELTDOWN: the floor is falling. Survive 15 seconds.
💠 COPY THAT: repeat the arrows. Bank it or go again.
🎯 LONG SHOT: aim, power, get fired out of a cannon at the rings.

Earn 24 medals, beat your best, play the daily 2x Hot Seat, and spend GUTS in THE VAULT on fail trails and launch bursts. All earned by playing.

Works solo. Friends just make the fails louder.

🧪 Live test build: progress, prices and balance may change.
