# How Much Does SEO Cost in Canada? (Real Numbers, 2026)

**In Canada, professional monthly SEO typically runs from about $500/month at the low end to $5,000+/month for large-scope agency engagements. StillAwake Media's plans are published: $600 CAD/month (Essential) and $850 CAD/month (Growth) — no 12-month contract.** One-time work like audits and cleanup projects is usually quoted per scope.

That first paragraph already makes this article unusual: almost no Canadian agency publishes pricing. In our research across the Montréal market, we found exactly one competitor with public SEO prices. Everyone else routes you into a sales call first — which tells you the price is set after they've sized *you*, not the work.

## Why SEO pricing varies so much

"SEO" describes wildly different amounts of labor. The honest way to think about cost is to ask what is actually being done each month:

| Monthly price range (CAD) | What it usually buys | Honest assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under $300 | Automated reports, directory submissions, maybe a plugin install | Rarely moves anything; often resold offshore work |
| $500 – $1,500 | A real practitioner: technical fixes, on-page work, content, local SEO, monthly reporting | The productive range for most small and mid-size businesses |
| $1,500 – $5,000 | Larger content programs, link acquisition, multiple specialists | Justified for competitive niches and larger sites |
| $5,000+ | Enterprise: dedicated teams, engineering integration | Overkill below serious scale |

These ranges are market observations from operating in this industry — not quotes. Any real engagement should give you a written scope that says exactly what happens each month.

## What actually determines the cost

Five factors drive almost all of the variance:

**1. Your starting point.** A technically broken site needs a cleanup phase before growth work makes sense. When we took over [TravelDesign By Lisa's platform](/work/lisa-travel-design), roughly 2,300 technical SEO defects had to be cleared before the rankings moved — that work is real, and it's why month one and month six of an engagement look different.

**2. Competition in your niche.** Ranking a specialty manufacturer in a small city and ranking a mortgage broker in Toronto are different problems. Competitive query sets need more content, more authority, more time.

**3. Language scope.** In Québec this is the big hidden variable: doing SEO properly in English *and* French roughly doubles the content surface. (It's also a genuine opportunity — the French side of most markets here is far less competitive. Our [French keyword research](/seo-montreal) is built into how we scope Québec work.)

**4. Content production.** Who writes the pages that rank? If content is included, the retainer covers real hours. If it isn't, budget for it separately or expect little movement.

**5. Whether AI-search work is included.** Optimizing for AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity — [AEO](/stillawake-times/what-is-generative-engine-optimization) — is increasingly part of serious SEO. Our Growth plan includes it; many retainers don't mention it at all.

## How long until SEO pays for itself?

The uncomfortable truth: technical fixes often show effects in weeks; competitive rankings usually take months. Any provider promising page 1 in 30 days is either targeting queries nobody searches or planning to disappoint you.

What a real timeline looks like, from our own measured work: Lisa's platform went from zero organic clicks to page-1 positions in 8 weeks — but that was with a full technical rebuild and a favorable niche. [Blackwater Aquatics](/work/blackwater-aquatics) took months of education-led content before its product pages ranked; it now gets weekly orders from organic search with zero ad spend, which is the actual point: SEO cost is a purchase of a durable asset, ads are a rental.

## Red flags that cost more than any retainer

- **Guaranteed rankings.** Nobody controls Google. A guarantee means cherry-picked keywords or refund roulette.
- **Secret methods.** Real SEO survives being explained. If they can't tell you what they'll do each month, they don't intend to do much.
- **12-month lock-ins before any results.** The contract replaces the results as the retention mechanism.
- **No access to your own Search Console.** Your data. Always. Walk away from anyone who won't share it.
- **Reports without actions.** Ranking dashboards are not deliverables. The report should say what was *done*.

## The bottom line

Expect to invest $500–$1,500/month for genuine SEO on a typical Canadian small-business site, more in competitive niches — and demand a written scope, your own data access, and no long lock-in at any price.
