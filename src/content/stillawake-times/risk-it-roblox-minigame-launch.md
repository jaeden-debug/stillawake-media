# RISK IT!: Our Second Roblox Game Is Eight Tiny Challenges and a Lot of Ragdolls

**RISK IT!** is StillAwake Media's second game on Roblox. You spawn on a neon hub floating over a synthwave void, with eight challenge islands around you. Walk onto a pad, press RISK IT!, and you get between five and twenty seconds to win. Win and you bank GUTS. Fail and the game makes a show of it, then puts you back at the pad for one more go.

It's in a live test now. Everything below is from build v0.2.2, captured in the game itself on 26 October 2026.

![The whole RISK IT! arena from above: the hub and eight neon challenge islands](/games/risk-it/branding/risk-it-arena-aerial.webp)
*The hub and its eight islands, one challenge each.*

## The eight challenges

Each challenge tests a different skill, and no two use the same input:

- **[THE BUTTON](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/the-button)**: press your luck. The pot doubles every press, and the board tells you the exact chance the next press drops you.
- **[PERFECT STOP](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/perfect-stop)**: stop a needle in a zone that shrinks over three rounds.
- **[QUICK DRAW](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/quick-draw)**: wait for green. More than half the rounds flash yellow first.
- **[THE SWEEPER](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/the-sweeper)**: cross a bridge past three spinning arms in 16 seconds.
- **[DOOR NUMBER WHO](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/door-number-who)**: three doors, one trap, and a lamp that gives it away if you watch.
- **[MELTDOWN](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/meltdown)**: survive 15 seconds on a floor that falls away in waves.
- **[COPY THAT](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/copy-that)**: repeat arrows, then bank or go again.
- **[LONG SHOT](/games/risk-it/wiki/challenges/long-shot)**: aim a cannon, lock the power, and get fired at a ring.

## Failure is the show

The idea behind RISK IT! is simple: failing should be the funniest part. Every challenge has its own gag. THE BUTTON drops you through a trapdoor, PERFECT STOP punches you with a spring-loaded boxing glove, QUICK DRAW catapults you, and COPY THAT's floor fires you straight up. A headline stamps across the screen, the camera chases you, and about 2.6 seconds later you're on your feet beside the pad, facing it, with the prompt already up.

![A player launched through the air with a rainbow trail and a starburst](/games/risk-it/branding/risk-it-launch-full-spectrum.webp)
*A QUICK DRAW flinch, wearing the Full Spectrum trail and the Seeing Stars burst.*

Wipeouts even count toward something: your title. You start as FRESH MEAT and, 400 wipeouts later, you're UNKILLABLE.

## Honest odds, nothing for sale

Every result is decided on Roblox's server from the rules [documented in the wiki](/games/risk-it/wiki), not on your device. THE BUTTON shows its real odds before every press. Timing challenges judge your press by when you pressed, with a small capped allowance for lag. And nothing you can buy changes any of it: [THE VAULT](/games/risk-it/wiki/vault/the-vault) sells fail trails and launch bursts, bought only with GUTS you earn by playing.

## Built for phones too

RISK IT! runs on computer, phone and tablet. We tested the layout in Roblox Studio's Device Simulator: the RISK IT! prompt is a big tap target, movement challenges dock their panel at the top so you can see the floor, and keyboard hints disappear on touch screens.

![RISK IT! on a phone: MELTDOWN with the challenge panel docked at the top](/games/risk-it/mobile/risk-it-mobile-meltdown.webp)
*MELTDOWN on a phone-sized screen.*

We haven't tested it on real phones yet, and saving progress on live servers is still being confirmed. Those are the two things this live test is for.
