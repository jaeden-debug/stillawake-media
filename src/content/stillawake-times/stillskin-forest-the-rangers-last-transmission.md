> **Pre-release draft: published at the V3 release.**

> **Short answer.** *The Ranger's Last Transmission* is the story campaign that V3 adds to [Stillskin Forest](/games/stillskin-forest), our co-op survival-horror game on Roblox. A radio briefing sends your crew into a closed wildlife reserve to find out what happened to its missing ranger. Over 22 chapters you follow her journals and tapes through the forest, the town of Stillwater, Blackthorn Asylum and a collapsed military quarantine, until her trail runs out somewhere it shouldn't. Then your crew makes a choice that changes the forest for good.

The first half of this article is safe to read before you play; the second tells the whole story, including the ending. Everything here comes from our V3 design documents, written before the update shipped, so some details may change. Game mechanics are marked *(to verify at release)* until we've checked them in the released build.

**Spoiler levels.** Everything up to the heading "Spoilers: the full story" is spoiler-free. Below that heading, nothing is held back. The FAQ at the end is spoiler-free again.

## The story so far (no spoilers)

### The reserve

Stillskin Wildlife Reserve sits in the hills above the town of Stillwater. It used to be busy: summer campers and families, hunters, a logging crew, an airfield and a ranger service to keep it all in order. Then the animals went. Not fewer of them; none. No tracks in the mud, no birdsong at the lake. Campers started reporting glowing eyes in the trees at night, sounds nothing should make, and voices they couldn't place.

One ranger stayed to find out why. She spent more than 100 days investigating, from the Ranger Base and the fire watchtower, writing everything down and recording what she couldn't write fast enough. Then she stopped reporting.

The reserve is closed now, "following a series of unexplained disappearances". You're the people sent in to find out what happened.

### How the campaign starts

A new session opens at Camp Stillskin in late-morning light, with no HUD prompts for a few seconds: just the forest, and nothing alive in it. Then the radio crackles and dispatch gives you the job: find out what happened, starting with the missing ranger's records at the Ranger Base and the fire watchtower. Dispatch names her as Ranger Hollis *(to verify at release)*.

Two objectives appear: **Find the ranger's field records** and **Prepare for nightfall** *(to verify at release)*. The first clue is the brightest thing in view, the ranger's notice on the camp board beside a lantern. It asks campers to report any animal sightings at all, warns them not to follow lights or voices off the marked trails, and ends with a newer line in pen: her records are at the base and in the tower.

From there, the campaign runs alongside the survival game you already know: scavenge by day, survive the night, keep your crew alive. The [campaign start guide](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/campaign-getting-started) covers the first hour.

### What you'll investigate

The ranger's journals read like a professional's at first: dated days, counts, tracks, weather. Each one points to the next place she went. Without giving anything away, the trail takes you through:

- **The Ranger Base and the fire watchtower**: her first entries, and her radio calls to a dispatch that never sent help.
- **The hunting camp**, where a frightened hunter everyone calls [Backstreet](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/backstreet) saw something, dropped his rifle and ran.
- **Stillwater**, where half the town went "up the road".
- **Green Lake and the Lost Camp**, following a green glow in the trees to a military prototype.
- **Blackthorn Asylum** and the Warden who ran it.
- **The four relics and the ritual**, an old rite that puts the thing under the hill to sleep.
- **Blackpine Airfield**, where the military's quarantine fell apart.
- **Her final transmissions.**

There are 22 chapters in all *(to verify at release)*. The [chapter overview](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/main-campaign) lists the first fifteen without spoilers.

### Journals, tapes and hints

The ranger's [journals](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/ranger-journals) are the backbone of the story: 25 to 35 entries, counting her tapes *(to verify at release)*. You'll also find recordings from dispatch, Backstreet, a Stillwater family, the asylum's staff and the military. There's no voice acting: every [recording](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/tapes-and-recordings) plays as an on-screen transcript over sound effects, and the text is saved in your journal *(to verify at release)*.

Nothing needs outside research. Every required clue has three hints: a line in an earlier clue, a marker in the world (a desk lamp left on, a recorder chirping by a fire), and, if you're stuck for 12 in-game hours, a short "Ranger's note" in your journal with an optional map pin *(to verify at release)*.

Order is a guide, not a gate. Every clue counts the moment anyone in your [saved session](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/multiplayer/persistent-expeditions) finds it, so reading something early never leaves you stuck, and dying or disconnecting never clears progress *(to verify at release)*.

### Night vision and the ritual

At some point you'll want to see in the dark. In V3, the only night-vision goggles in the campaign are trapped in the ritual circle in the asylum's cafeteria: you can see a faint green glint at its centre, but your hand stops short of it. Completing the [Rite of Stillness](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/quests/the-rite-of-stillness) releases them, and each survivor claims their own pair *(to verify at release)*. You'll need them for the later chapters. The [night vision guide](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/night-vision-and-the-ritual) explains how the trail leads there.

### Something is watching

Early in the campaign, usually when one of you is alone and busy, you may look up and see a tall, pale figure at the tree line, perfectly still, staring at you *(to verify at release)*. It doesn't chase. It doesn't attack. Look away and it's gone. Nobody else saw it. These are the [Watchers](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/watchers), and they come back.

A few nights later, the forest goes unnaturally quiet. The infected gather at the lamp posts and circle them as if they're listening to something, and they ignore you. This is the campaign's first [Silent Night](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/silent-night-story), and it arrives between Days 4 and 6 *(to verify at release)*.

### How it ends, without spoilers

The campaign has three endings, and every one of them is permanent for the session. None is a victory screen followed by a reset: the session carries on in the world your crew chose *(to verify at release)*. Your crew votes on the choice, and the button labels never tell you what will happen. Two of the choices are "Follow the light" and "Refuse and run". A third choice appears only for crews who prepared for it.

## Spoilers: the full story

### What really happened

Something old lives under and around the forest. Stillwater's people called it the Hollow. It controls bodies through a signal the asylum's Warden called "the frequency", and the infected are what it does to people when it holds them long enough.

The Warden, Dr. H. Vance, took Stillwater's sick into Blackthorn Asylum, studied the frequency, ran experiments and stole a pair of night-vision goggles from the military. Sister Agnes's old rite was meant to put the thing to sleep; the Warden believed he could use it to take control. One night he walked into the cafeteria circle with the whole ward singing and the goggles over his eyes. Something answered. His consciousness is still trapped inside it, and his goggles are still in the circle. In our current draft, this happened before the ranger arrived *(to verify at release)*.

The ranger found all of it, one entry at a time. Late on, she heard her own voice on the Green Lake shore, eight kilometres from where she stood, calling for help in words she hadn't said yet. Her last tape, left in the Lookout Camp tent, says:

> "It learns voices. It learned mine. If you hear my voice telling you it's safe, don't believe me. I'd never tell you that. Not here."

She found a tree past the end of the map, walked up to it on purpose, and went through. She died on the other side, in the Astral. To everyone else she simply disappeared; in our current draft, her disappearance and her entering the Astral are the same event *(to verify at release)*.

After she vanished, thrill-seeking campers met the infected and called for help: police, then the military. The quarantine collapsed. A containment specialist escaped with the freeze prototype as far as Green Lake; a camper carried it on to the Lost Camp.

Then the entity did something patient. It took the genuine trail the ranger, the Warden and the military had left, and arranged it: which records you'd find, in what order, and where they'd lead. One relic was moved, its new spot marked on her fire map in green ink that isn't her handwriting. Every clue you follow is real. The question is why you found it.

### The Watchers

The Watchers are the entity's observers. They watched the ranger, and they've been watching your expedition from the first day. In the forest they only stare; each appearance is staged, and the musical phrase that warns you of them changes each time, from unfamiliar to recognisable to distorted *(to verify at release)*. On Silent Night one stands where your whole crew can see it.

Once you've heard the ranger's final tapes and lived through Silent Night, the Watchers come for you. They appear on every side of the tree line one night, closer than ever, and your crew collapses for a few seconds without taking damage *(to verify at release)*. You wake together in the cave tomb, beside a torn page from her fire map. On its back: "If you woke up in here, you didn't walk in. I'm sorry. Keep going."

### The Dark Forest and the Nowhere Tree

The page's route leads north-east into the [Dark Forest](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/dark-forest), near-black and full of whispers. A lamp lights about a metre there, which is why the ritual's goggles matter *(to verify at release)*. The ranger walked this way without them, past a ranger-service trail marker where nobody ever put a trail.

At the end is the [Nowhere Tree](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/nowhere-tree). The whispers stop. Through a gap in its roots there's grey, like fog in daylight. Her last recording on this side is at the roots: "I said I'd only look. I'm going to look." Then footsteps, then nothing. Everyone present has to choose to go through; nobody is forced *(to verify at release)*.

### The Astral

  <img src="/games/stillskin-forest/locations/stillskin-forest-green-lake.webp" alt="Blackthorn Asylum above Green Lake at night, with infected along the fence" width="1672" height="941" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>Green Lake, where the green light is strongest above ground, and where the ranger first heard her own voice.</figcaption>
</figure>

[The Astral](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/the-astral) is a world of grey fog that night vision can't cut, bare trees, sharp dry ground and ruined houses. There are no infected. Your crew has to survive one full night there, on one of three routes chosen by a saved seed *(to verify at release)*.

Here the Watchers are hostile. One can grab a survivor and drain their health; the Freeze Device (about ten seconds), fighting back or struggling free breaks the grab *(to verify at release)*. Teammates never hurt each other. If everyone falls, your crew wakes at the cave tomb checkpoint and can try again *(to verify at release)*.

By a ruined house on every route lies the ranger's lantern, cold, its glass cracked. Scratched into the brass with a knife point: **"R.H. — it is not me."** It's the truest clue in the game, and the one the entity didn't arrange. She left it in spite of it.

Near dawn a distant light appears, and her lost handheld radio crackles: "Hello? Is someone there? Oh, thank God. I can see your lights. Come towards the light. It's safe now."

### The figure in the light

The ranger is standing in the light: uniform, lamp, alive. She doesn't move, and her voice comes from the radio on her vest, not her mouth. She says you must be exhausted, and that "there will be a sensible answer. There usually is", word for word the end of her Day 1 journal. She says it's safe now, and offers to walk you back through the tree together. If you hesitate: "It's safe. It's safe now."

She isn't the ranger. The real ranger died in the Astral, and this is the entity wearing her voice and shape. It never gives a day number or mentions her warning tape, the Warden's notes or the lantern. It wants survivors who accept its influence and carry it out of the Astral.

### The three endings

All three are permanent. Everyone present votes; the majority wins, a tie gets one re-vote, and a second tie means your crew runs *(to verify at release)*. Members who weren't there are bound by the result.

**The Marked: "Follow the light."** You accept the voice. She leads you back through the tree and you wake in the original forest, changed. You move faster, your stamina lasts longer, and revives and healing are quicker, but the infected are drawn to you, and the lamp-post circles tighten *(to verify at release)*. Dispatch reports survivors "moving through the reserve faster than anyone should". You carried it out. [More on the Marked](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/ending-the-marked).

**The Remains: "Refuse and run."** You turn your back on the light and run through the grey. You wake at Green Lake in a burned, desolate copy of the whole forest, with no gifts, scarce resources and no way back *(to verify at release)*. Nothing followed you. Nothing is left. Dispatch can barely reach you. [More on the Remains](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/ending-the-remains).

**The Severance: "Destroy the false ranger."** This choice appears only if your crew has the ranger's warning tape, the Warden's frequency notes, and a member present who carries the Freeze Device *(to verify at release)*. The Warden's notes are what make it possible: he found that the green light interrupts the frequency, and that "any voice that has spoken near it can be spoken again". The device's green light cuts the voice off mid-word, and for those seconds the figure stands silent and wrong. The ritual isn't required, but it gives your crew an advantage in the confrontation *(to verify at release)*. You wake in a damaged, quieter forest with no curse and no gift, where the lamp-post circles are rare and broken *(to verify at release)*. Dispatch says it's too early to call it over. Something may still be under the hill. [More on the Severance](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/ending-the-severance).

The [post-campaign guide](/games/stillskin-forest/wiki/campaign/post-campaign-survival) compares the three worlds side by side.

### How to see it coming

We wanted the twist to be deducible, not just surprising. On a Stillwater answering machine, a mother's two frightened messages are followed by her son's, calm and warm: "It's safe now. Come outside." Every real recording of the ranger opens with a day number or a place. Her warning tape tells you what the false ranger will say. Players who found it should feel the recognition before the choice appears; players who didn't, a relief that turns wrong later.

## FAQ

### What is The Ranger's Last Transmission?

It's the story campaign added to Stillskin Forest in the V3 update. Your crew investigates a closed wildlife reserve and the missing ranger who spent more than 100 days studying it, following her journals and tapes across 22 chapters to a permanent ending your crew chooses (to verify at release).

### How does the Stillskin Forest campaign start?

Every new session opens at Camp Stillskin with a radio briefing from dispatch. Your first objectives are to find the ranger's field records and to prepare for nightfall, and the first clue is her notice on the camp board (to verify at release).

### How do you get night vision in the campaign?

Complete the Rite of Stillness in Blackthorn Asylum's cafeteria. The Warden's night-vision goggles are trapped in the ritual circle, and finishing the ritual releases them; each survivor claims their own pair (to verify at release).

### What are the Watchers?

Tall, pale figures that observe your crew. In the forest they stare and vanish without attacking. In the Astral they are hostile and can grab a survivor, and the grab can be broken with the Freeze Device, by fighting back or by struggling free (to verify at release).

### What happens if everyone dies in the Astral?

The session doesn't end. Everyone wakes at the cave tomb checkpoint and can try again. If you leave mid-Astral, you rejoin the live Astral when teammates are still there, or the checkpoint when they aren't (to verify at release).

### How many endings does Stillskin Forest have?

Three: the Marked, the Remains and the Severance. All three are permanent for the session, and your crew votes on the choice. The button labels never reveal what happens (to verify at release).

### How do you unlock the Severance ending?

Your crew needs three things before the final choice: the ranger's last tape from the Lookout Camp tent, the Warden's frequency notes from the locked cabinet in his office, and a member present who carries the Freeze Device (its cooldown doesn't matter). The ritual isn't required, but it helps (to verify at release).

### Can you change your ending afterwards?

No. The ending is permanent for that session, and the session carries on in the world your crew chose. To see another ending, start a new session (to verify at release).

### Does the campaign have voice acting?

No. Every tape, radio call and answering machine appears as an on-screen transcript with sound effects, and the text is saved in your journal (to verify at release).
